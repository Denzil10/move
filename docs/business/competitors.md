## Move Pet's Edge (The Wedge)

1. **Physical Verification:** Most break apps are easily dismissed with a click. Move Pet requires actual movement (via webcam frame-diff) to satisfy the pet.
2. **Emotional Accountability:** The pet doesn't just "remind" you; it looks disturbed/sad when you are inactive and happy when you move. This leverages the "Tamagotchi effect" for health.
3. **Strict Mode:** Optional hard-lock of inputs for users who truly struggle with discipline.
4. **Privacy-First:** Unlike some "wellness" apps that might track screen time or usage, Move Pet's motion detection is entirely local.

## Pricing Hypothesis

- **Freemium:** Core pet, inactivity timer, and basic motion detection are free.
- **Pro ($5-10 one-time or small annual):**
    - Strict Mode (Hard accountability).
    - Advanced Analytics (Calorie history, streak tracking).
    - Custom Themes/Skins (Different pets: Cat, Slime, Robot).
    - Discord/Slack Integration (Share streaks).
