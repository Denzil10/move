## 2. itch.io Launch Assets

**Target Audience:** Gamers, streamers, and indie-lovers who appreciate cozy companions and gamified health tools.

### Visual Assets (itch.io)
- **Cover Image:** 630x500 
  - *Content:* Cute, high-contrast illustration of the pet in its "Joyful" state with weather effects (e.g., sunny rays).
- **Screenshots:** 
  - 1. Pet hovering over a popular game UI (e.g., Stardew Valley or Minecraft).
  - 2. The Customization Shop showing different hats, collars, and colors.
  - 3. The Quest Journal showing daily movement tasks.
  - 4. The Daily Recap showing calories burned and "Pet Dreams".
- **Gameplay Trailer:** 30-45 seconds.
  - *Format:* Fast-paced, focusing on the RPG mechanics, the weather changing, leveling up, and unlocking items. Lo-fi or cozy chiptune backing track.

### Textual Assets (itch.io)
- **Title:** Move Pet
- **Short Description:** A cozy desktop companion that helps you stay active while gaming.
- **Genre:** Simulation / Health
- **Tags:** Virtual Pet, Cozy, Productivity, Gamification, 2D, Singleplayer
- **Long Description:**
  > **Don't let your health bar drop in real life.**
  > 
  > Move Pet is a lightweight virtual companion that lives on your desktop. Whether you're in a marathon gaming session or grinding out code, Move Pet keeps track of your inactivity. Sit for too long, and your pet gets grumpy. Stand up, stretch, and move around to make them happy!
  > 
  > **Features:**
  > - **Your Personal Movement Buddy:** Choose your pet's personality and watch them react to your activity levels.
  > - **Gamify Your Health:** Earn XP, level up, and collect Pet Coins by moving.
  > - **Customize:** Use coins to buy accessories, treats, and mystery boxes from the shop.
  > - **Deep RPG Mechanics:** Manage their Hunger, Energy, and Mood. Build a lasting Friendship Bond.
  > - **Dynamic World:** Your pet reacts to local weather, seasons, and even has unique dreams when they sleep.
  > - **Privacy Safe:** All movement detection is processed locally. We don't want your data!
  > 
  > *Available now for Windows and Linux (macOS coming soon).*
