# Move Pet Marketing Brief

## Target Audience
- **Deep-Work Builders:** Coders, writers, and designers who lose track of time.
- **Dedicated Gamers:** Players who spend hours in competitive or immersive sessions.
- **Passive Scrollers:** People who spend hours on social media without moving.

## Core Value Proposition
"Move Pet is the tiny desktop companion that gets upset when you sit too long—and happy when you finally move."

Unlike generic notification popups that are easy to ignore or dismiss, Move Pet provides emotional feedback and visual accountability. It doesn't just remind you; it *cares* about your health.

## Landing Page Structure (MVP)

### 1. Hero Section
- **Headline:** Stop sitting still. Start moving with your pet.
- **Sub-headline:** A lightweight desktop pet that motivates you to stay active during long work and gaming sessions.
- **Visual:** A GIF or short loop of the pet going from "Disturbed" (darker, slightly smoking) to "Happy" (bright, floating).
- **CTA:** Join the Waitlist (Email field).

### 2. How it Works
- **The Inactivity Trigger:** After 3 hours (configurable) of inactivity, your pet appears on your screen.
- **The Emotional Loop:** It stays unhappy until you move. It uses local webcam frame-diffing to detect motion—no frames ever leave your device.
- **The Reward:** When you move, the pet gets happy, floats with you for 3 seconds, and disappears with a victory toast showing estimated calories burned.
- **Strict Mode (Optional):** For those who need hard accountability, lock your inputs until you meet your movement target.

### 3. Privacy First
- **Local Only:** All motion detection happens in your browser/Tauri shell.
- **No Storage:** No images or videos are stored or sent anywhere.
- **Transparent:** Open-source core so you can verify our privacy claims.

### 4. Join the Journey
- Waitlist for macOS Alpha.
- Link to Discord/Twitter.

## Content Concepts (Social Media)

### Concept A: "The Guilt Trip"
- A 15-second clip of a dev coding intensely.
- Zoom in on the screen edge where the pet appears, looking increasingly annoyed/disturbed.
- Caption: "When your pet knows you've been sitting for 4 hours straight..."

### Concept B: "The Victory Dance"
- A clip of someone doing a quick stretch or 5 jumping jacks.
- The pet suddenly lights up, hearts appear, and it floats around the screen.
- Victory toast: "5 Minutes Moved! 🔥 25 Calories Estimated."

### Concept C: "Strict Mode Stakes"
- A gamer in the middle of a match. The pet appears.
- Suddenly, a subtle red border appears on the screen (Strict Mode).
- Caption: "Strict Mode: Your pet won't let you queue for another match until you move."
