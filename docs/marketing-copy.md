# Move Pet Marketing Copy & Ad Hooks

This document contains refined marketing copy and ad hooks for the Move Pet launch.

## 1. Ad Hooks (Short & Punchy)

### Hook 1: The Guilt Trip
- **Text**: "This tiny dragon is judging you for sitting too long. 🐉"
- **Visual**: Pet in "Disturbed" state with smoke effects next to a coding window.
- **Angle**: Emotional accountability.

### Hook 2: The Reward
- **Text**: "Make your pet happy. Get yourself moving. 💖"
- **Visual**: Transition from "Disturbed" to "Happy" (hearts/floating) when user stands up.
- **Angle**: Positive reinforcement.

### Hook 3: The Privacy Promise
- **Text**: "Health tracking that actually respects your privacy. 100% local webcam detection. 🔒"
- **Visual**: Privacy indicator in status panel + "No frames leave this device" text.
- **Angle**: Security-conscious users.

### Hook 4: The Gamer's Companion
- **Text**: "Don't let your streak die. Move before your next match. 🔥"
- **Visual**: Pet appearing over a gaming UI (e.g., League or Valorant).
- **Angle**: Gamification and habit building.

## 2. Refined Website Copy

### Hero Header
**Your Desktop Pet is Judging Your Posture.**
*Stop ignoring generic reminders. Meet the companion that actually cares if you move.*

### Product Description
Move Pet is a lightweight desktop app designed for deep-work builders and dedicated gamers. It sits quietly on your screen until you've been inactive for too long—then it gets "Disturbed." The only way to make it happy? Stand up and move.

### Feature Highlights
- **Emotional Feedback**: See your pet evolve from idle to disturbed to ecstatic.
- **Privacy-First Motion**: Uses local webcam frame-diffing. No video, no cloud, no leaks.
- **RPG Progression**: Burn calories to level up your pet, earn coins, and unlock accessories.
- **Daily Quests & Friendship**: Build a bond with your pet through daily movement quests, and watch your Friendship level soar.
- **Pet Dreams & Journals**: Keep track of your pet's sleep habits and personality-driven dreams in the Dream Journal.
- **Weather & Seasons**: Dynamic local weather and seasonal changes that affect your pet's mood and reward multipliers.
- **Smart Focus & Quiet Hours**: Deep-work timer with breathing exercises, and automatic timer pausing when you are focused on productivity apps.
- **Strict Mode**: Optional screen locking for those who need a serious nudge.
