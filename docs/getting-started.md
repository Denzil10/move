# Getting Started with Move Pet

Move Pet is your digital companion designed to help you stay active, mindful, and engaged during long sessions at your computer. Whether you're gaming, coding, or working, your pet will remind you to move when you've been inactive for too long.

## The Core Loop

1.  **Inactivity**: Your pet stays hidden while you're focused. After a configurable period of inactivity (default 3 hours), it appears in a **Disturbed** state (with smoke effects!).
2.  **Movement**: Once the pet appears, it's time to move! Stand up, stretch, or do some jumping jacks. Your webcam will detect your motion. A blue **Movement Progress Bar** will appear above your pet as you move, showing how much closer you are to your goal.
3.  **Reward**: As you move, your pet becomes **Happy** and floats with you. If you meet your movement goal, you'll receive a **Victory Toast** with estimated calories burned and earn **Pet Coins** and **XP**.
4.  **Growth**: Every movement session earns you XP. Level up your pet to unlock new visual evolutions (like wings and the Titan Dragon stage!).

## Pet Care & Interactivity

Your pet is a living digital companion with its own needs:

*   **Hunger & Energy**: Your pet gets hungry and tired over time. Restore energy via the **Pet Sleep Schedule** (auto-sleep/wake or manual Bedtime) or feeding it from your **Inventory** keeps it healthy.
*   **Mood**: Mood affects your pet's patience. A **Joyful** pet will wait longer before getting disturbed. Boost mood by interacting with your pet—try **Petting**, **Poking**, or **Chatting**!
*   **Needs Dashboard**: Keep an eye on the dedicated dashboard to monitor Hunger, Energy, and Mood.
*   **Friendship**: Build a bond with your pet through consistent care. Higher friendship levels provide XP and Coin multipliers. Check your friendship progress in the **Pet Journal**.
*   **Pet Dreams**: During sleep, your pet may have dreams based on its personality. Record them in the **Pet Diary** to track memories!
*   **Pet Diary**: Record daily memories and notes about your pet's journey together.

## Quests & Economy

*   **Pet Coins**: Earn coins through movement, leveling up, and daily login bonuses.
*   **Consumables Shop**: Spend coins on Treats, Energy Drinks, or Mystery Boxes to get random gifts and items.
*   **Quests**: Check the **Quest Journal** for active challenges (like "Window Shopper" or "Power Nap"). Complete Daily Quests for bonus rewards, or spend coins to reroll them.
*   **Pet Training**: Focus on attributes like **Strength Training** to improve calorie burn efficiency and unlock powerful new forms.
*   **Achievements**: Unlock achievements for milestones like care streaks, friendship levels, and calorie goals. Share your progress with the **Share My Progress** feature!

## Environment & Mindfulness

*   **Weather & Seasons**: Your pet reacts to the local weather and seasons! Expect unique particle effects (rain, snow, sun rays) and reward multipliers (e.g., 1.5x Coins/XP on snowy days).
*   **Breathing Exercise (4-7-8)**: Need to de-stress? Initiate a guided breathing exercise for focus and relaxation.
*   **Daily Recap**: Get a summary of yesterday's stats with personality-specific dialogue. Perfect for starting your day!
*   **Pause Tracking**: If you're stepping away or going into a deep focus session, you can temporarily disable the inactivity timer using the Pause Tracking feature.

## Productivity Features

*   **Smart Focus**: Enable this mode to automatically pause tracking when focused apps (like IDEs, design tools) are active. Get break suggestions after a configurable interval, and earn a **Focus Zen** buff (25% reward bonus) after 25 minutes of continuous focus!
*   **Quiet Hours**: Set specific time windows (with separate weekend schedules) when tracking is automatically paused. Perfect for meetings or family time.
*   **App Usage Report**: See which focused apps pause tracking most often to understand your work patterns.
*   **Mini Mode**: Toggle a compact view that hides stats and settings for a less distracting desktop companion.
*   **Always on Top**: Pin your pet window above all other applications so it's always visible.
*   **Pet Opacity**: Adjust the transparency of your pet for a subtle desktop presence.

## Configuration Tips

To make the most of Move Pet, visit the **Settings** panel:

### 1. Motion Sensitivity
*   **Low Score (e.g., 1.0)**: More sensitive. Good if your camera is far away or dim.
*   **High Score (e.g., 3.0)**: Less sensitive. Good if there's background movement.
*   *Tip*: Use the **Motion Meter** in the Status panel to see how your movements translate to scores.

### 2. Strict Mode
*   **What it does**: Locks your keyboard and mouse when the pet is disturbed. You *must* move to regain control.
*   **Safety**: There is a "Hold to Escape" emergency exit if you need to bypass it quickly.

### 3. Daily Goals
*   Set a **Daily Move Goal** and a **Daily Calorie Goal**. Track your progress via progress bars and the **Weekly Activity Report**.

### 4. Pet Customization
*   **Pet Name**: Give your pet a custom name.
*   **Pet Color**: Choose your pet's color.
*   **Personality**: Select from personalities like Cheerful, Lazy, Adventurous, or Stoic. Each has unique dialogue and gameplay modifiers!
*   **Species**: Unlock new pet species by reaching milestones (calories, streaks, levels, seasons).
*   **Accessories**: Unlock collars, hats, and more with Pet Coins.

### 5. Display Options
*   **Theme**: Choose between Light, Dark, or System theme.
*   **Mini Mode**: Toggle compact view.
*   **Always on Top**: Keep pet above other windows.
*   **Pet Opacity**: Adjust transparency (0.2 to 1.0).

## Privacy

*   **Local Processing**: All motion detection happens entirely on your device.
*   **No Recording**: Move Pet never saves or transmits your camera feed.
*   **Indicator**: A privacy indicator appears in the app when the camera is active.

## Tips for Success

1.  **Start Small**: Begin with a shorter inactivity timeout (1-2 hours) to build the habit.
2.  **Use Smart Focus**: If you work with focused apps, enable Smart Focus to avoid interruptions during deep work.
3.  **Care for Your Pet**: Keep hunger and energy levels high for better mood and patience.
4.  **Complete Quests**: Daily quests are an easy way to earn extra coins and XP.
5.  **Check the Journal**: The Pet Journal tracks milestones, dreams, friendship progress, and wellness reports.
6.  **Share Progress**: Use the "Share My Progress" feature to generate social sharing images and celebrate your achievements!
