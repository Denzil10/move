Move Pet System

Regular movement, made hard to ignore.

A tiny desktop pet appears at intervals, gives one fun movement prompt, verifies local motion, celebrates, and gets out of the way.

Architecture And Logic

One compact desktop loop: interval, prompt, pet animation, local motion score, reward.

1

Prompt Timer

Configurable intervals choose short movement prompts: march, stretch, squats, shoulder rolls, water walk.

2

Pet Overlay

Transparent always-on-top Tauri window shows a bundled Hatch/Codex-style pet pack.

3

Motion Check

Browser webcam frame-diff scores movement locally; frames are discarded immediately.

4

Reward Loop

Three seconds of movement makes the pet happy; full target shows streak, calories, and victory.
